Summary

CVLY does not collect, send or share your data. Everything you write in your resume is stored only on your device.

What data CVLY stores

The content of your resume (name, contact details, experience, education, skills, photo and appearance settings) is stored locally on your iPhone. There is no account, registration or sign-in.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal or usage data.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking services.
  • We do not send your resume to our servers or to any third party.

Photo

If you choose a profile photo, the app accesses your photo library or camera only at that moment, at your request. The image is stored on your device, inside your resume.

Sharing and PDF

When you export or share a PDF or a backup, the file is created on your device and sent wherever you choose through the iOS share sheet. That sharing is controlled by you.

Deleting your data

You can delete your resume inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data from your device.

Children

CVLY does not collect data from anyone, including minors.
